How to Use This Tool

Step 1 — Global Parameters: Set capital (₹1 Cr default for Indian, USDT for crypto), max risk per trade (1%), max drawdown (15%), and current DD.

Step 2 — Trade Setup: Pick instrument and direction. System auto-fills lot size, margin, and default SL%. Enter your entry price.

Step 3 — Position Size: DD-Adjusted is recommended — linearly scales down as DD deepens, reaching zero at max DD.

Step 4 — Payoff Analysis: Crypto = futures P&L only. Indian = futures + options (Debit Spread, DS+Runner, or Deep ITM).

Step 5 — Scenario Matrix: Full P&L heatmap across market moves × holding days.

Options Strategies

Debit Spread (2 legs)

Buy ATM + Sell OTM. Defined risk (net debit), capped reward. Best for small–medium moves. Lowest cost option structure.

DS + Runner (3 legs)

Debit Spread + further OTM buy. Defined risk with unlimited upside beyond runner. Captures big regime moves.

Deep ITM Option (1 leg)

Buy deep ITM (delta 0.80–0.95). Near-futures exposure with built-in loss floor. No overnight conversion needed.

Fund-Level Risk Rules
● Max risk/trade: 1% of capital
● Max DD: 15% → halt trading
● DD-adjusted sizing: linear to zero
● Overnight: options only (Indian)
● Crypto: futures only, 5× leverage
● Single instrument: max 40% of risk
● Margin warning: 50%+
● Lot sizes: Zerodha (Mar 2026)
