Strategy Overview

Equiquant Capital employs a systematic Continuous Flow Regime Switch Long/Short Strategy focused on large-cap cryptocurrency and derivatives markets, primarily Bitcoin. The model identifies regime transitions across spot, perpetual futures, and options markets through proprietary flow analysis, dynamically switching between long and short exposure as conditions evolve.

The strategy operates on a medium-frequency basis across Bitcoin and select large-cap crypto derivatives, holding positions from hours to days depending on regime persistence. Risk management is fully integrated — every position is sized as a fixed percentage of capital with predefined stop-loss levels, ensuring consistent risk per trade regardless of entry price or market volatility.

Capital preservation is paramount. The strategy aims for asymmetric payoffs through disciplined entry criteria and systematic regime detection across the large-cap crypto universe, avoiding discretionary overrides. All signals are generated algorithmically with zero manual intervention during live trading.

Risk Framework

Each trade risks a fixed percentage of current capital (default 1%). Stop-losses are calculated automatically based on the configured stop-loss percentage from entry. Position sizing is derived from the risk amount divided by the distance to stop-loss, ensuring uniform risk exposure across all trades.

Monthly returns shown in the Performance tab are calculated as a percentage of starting capital at the time of each trade, reflecting true capital-weighted performance rather than simple P&L sums.
