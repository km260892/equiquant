Strategy Overview

Equiquant Technologies employs a systematic Continuous Flow Regime Switch Long/Short Strategy focused on large-cap cryptocurrency and derivatives markets, primarily Bitcoin. The model identifies regime transitions across spot, perpetual futures, and options markets through proprietary flow analysis.

The strategy operates on a medium-frequency basis, holding positions from hours to days depending on regime persistence. Risk management is fully integrated — every position is sized as a fixed percentage of capital with predefined stop-loss levels.

Capital preservation is paramount. All signals are generated algorithmically with zero manual intervention during live trading.

Sharpe Ratio Methodology

The Sharpe ratio is computed using per-trade returns, annualized by the empirically observed average holding period. For a mid-frequency strategy with variable holding durations, we compute N = 8766 / avg_holding_hours. Sharpe = (mean / std) × √N. Risk-free rate is zero given the crypto context and short holding periods.
