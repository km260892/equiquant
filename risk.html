How to Use This Tool

Step 1 — Global Parameters: Set capital (₹1 Cr default for Indian, USDT for crypto), max risk per trade (1%), max drawdown (15%), and current DD.

Step 2 — Trade Setup: Pick instrument and direction. System auto-fills lot size, margin, and default SL%. Enter your entry price.

Step 3 — Position Size: DD-Adjusted is recommended — linearly scales down as DD deepens, reaching zero at max DD.

Step 4 — Payoff Analysis: Crypto = futures P&L only. Indian = futures + options (Debit Spread, DS+Runner, Deep ITM, or Futures+OTM Hedge).

Step 5 — Portfolio Risk: Add active positions across all instruments. See total risk, margin, concentration, and worst-case analysis across your entire book. Alerts fire when you breach fund-level limits.

Options Strategies

Debit Spread (2 legs)

Buy ATM + Sell OTM. Defined risk (net debit), capped reward. Best for small–medium moves. Lowest cost option structure.

DS + Runner (3 legs)

Debit Spread + further OTM buy. Defined risk with unlimited upside beyond runner. Captures big regime moves.

Deep ITM Option (1 leg)

Buy deep ITM (delta 0.80–0.95). Near-futures exposure with built-in loss floor. No overnight conversion needed. Max loss = premium paid.

Futures + OTM Hedge (2 legs)

Hold full futures position + Buy OTM protective option (put for longs, call for shorts). Hedge distance auto-calculates to 80% of your SL, snapped to the nearest available strike. Full delta-1 exposure with unlimited upside, loss capped at hedge distance + premium. Best for: overnight gap protection while keeping futures speed during market hours.

Rolling Collar (3 legs) ★ DEFAULT

Futures + Buy OTM protective option (80% of SL) + Sell OTM call/put at SAME distance (symmetrical → near zero cost). When price moves 50% of collar distance in your favor, roll both legs up/down to the new price level. Each roll locks in profit while maintaining protection. Best for: all regimes. In choppy markets the collected premium buffers small-loss flips. In trending markets the rolling mechanism captures gains in steps without capping total upside.

OTM Hedge logic: Always buy nearest available expiry for the protective option — even 1-2 DTE. This is the cheapest possible overnight insurance. Roll it fresh each morning at market open. A 2% OTM Nifty weekly put at 1 DTE costs ₹5-15 vs ₹35-50 at 7 DTE — massive cost savings over a month.
Collar logic: Both legs (protective + sold) in the same expiry, 5-7 DTE for Nifty weeklies, 15-20 DTE for MCX monthlies. The sold option needs enough DTE to collect meaningful premium. Close entire collar on signal flip and rebuild in new direction.
DS / Runner logic: Debit spread in current or next week expiry. Runner leg in further-out expiry (next week or monthly) to give big moves time to develop.
General rule: Holding period should be ≤ 50% of remaining DTE. If a trade might last 3 days, buy options with at least 6 DTE.
